# What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally 
require human intelligence, such as making decisions, recognizing images, understanding language, and solving
 problems.

# What is Machine Learning (ML)?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of Artificial Intelligence that allows computers to learn from data and improve 
their performance without being explicitly programmed for every task.

1. Supervised Learning
The model learns from labeled data (input + correct output).
It is used to make predictions or classifications.

Examples:

Email spam detection
House price prediction
Student exam result prediction
2. Unsupervised Learning
The model learns from unlabeled data.
It finds hidden patterns or groups in the data.

Examples:

Customer segmentation
Product recommendation
Grouping similar documents
3. Reinforcement Learning
The model learns by trial and error.
It receives rewards for correct actions and penalties for wrong actions.

Examples:

Self-driving cars
Robot navigation
Game-playing AI (such as AlphaGo)

# What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning is an advanced branch of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks to process large amounts
 of data. It is widely used in image recognition, speech recognition, and natural language processing.

# What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of Artificial Intelligence that can create new content such as text, images, code, music, and
 videos based on the data it has learned from.

Examples of Generative AI:
- ChatGPT
- Google Gemini

# Real-World Examples

- Artificial Intelligence (AI): Google Maps suggests the best route based on traffic conditions.
- Machine Learning (ML): Netflix recommends movies and TV shows based on your viewing history.
- Deep Learning: Face Unlock on smartphones recognizes your face to unlock the device.
- Generative AI: ChatGPT can generate code, write articles, answer questions, and create content.
